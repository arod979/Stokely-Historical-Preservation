Overview

The Stokely Universe is a long-term historical preservation project designed to preserve, organize, evaluate, and communicate the legacy of Scott Stokely and the history surrounding the growth of disc golf.

This repository contains the governing documentation, architectural specifications, and institutional standards that define how the Stokely Universe operates.

The goal is not simply to create an archive.

The goal is to create a museum-ready historical knowledge system capable of preserving:

- Original resources
- Historical context
- Evidence
- Relationships
- Meaning
- Research questions
- Verified historical understanding

The Stokely Universe is designed to support:

- Scott Stokely's legacy preservation
- The "Growing Up Disc Golf" autobiography project
- Future museum exhibitions
- Educational resources
- Historical research
- Future acquisition of additional collections related to disc golf history

---

Foundational Philosophy

The Stokely Universe follows these principles:

Institution First, Technology Second

The system is designed to outlive any specific:

- Software platform
- Database
- Cloud provider
- AI model
- Individual contributor

Technology serves the institution. The institution does not serve the technology.

---

Preserve Evidence, Not Just Conclusions

The Stokely Universe preserves not only what is believed to be true, but also:

- Why it is believed
- What evidence supports it
- Who provided the information
- How certain the source was
- What remains unknown

Historical understanding must always be traceable back to evidence.

---

Human Historical Judgment Remains Final

Artificial intelligence may assist with:

- Discovery
- Organization
- Transcription
- Analysis
- Pattern recognition
- Research assistance

AI does not replace human judgment regarding:

- Historical significance
- Truth claims
- Interpretation
- Public presentation

---

Preserve Uncertainty

Unknown information is valuable.

The Stokely Universe does not guess, fill gaps, or create false certainty.

Unknowns become:

- Research questions
- Future investigations
- Opportunities for discovery

- Publications
- Interviews
- Websites
- Organizations

Examples:

- Photos
- Videos

Examples:

- Discs

Collections

Interviews

Narrative Episodes

Themes

---

Knowledge Graph

The Stokely Universe treats relationships as first-class historical records.

Connections between entities must include:

- Relationship type
- Supporting evidence
- Confidence level
- Review status
- Historical context
- Audit history

The system does not simply store information.

It preserves the connections that create historical understanding.

---

Evidence Model

The Stokely Universe separates three important concepts:

Statement Type

What kind of statement was made?

Examples:

- Observation
- Memory
- Opinion
- Interpretation
- Speculation
- Hearsay

---

Speaker Certainty

How confident was the person expressing the statement?

Examples:

- Explicit certainty
- Strong confidence
- Moderate confidence
- Qualified memory
- Speculation

---

Historian Confidence

After evaluating all evidence, how much confidence should the institution place in the conclusion?

Examples:

- Confirmed
- High
- Moderate
- Low
- Unknown
- Disputed

These measurements must never be combined.

A confident memory may be incorrect.
A cautious memory may be accurate.

Governance

The Stokely Universe uses a role-based responsibility model.

Responsibilities belong to roles, not individuals.

Initial roles include:

- Collector
- Archivist
- Historian
- Researcher
- Curator
- Publisher
- Administrator

People and AI systems may fulfill roles, but the responsibilities remain constant.

---

AI Governance

AI is considered a research and operations assistant.

AI may:

- Identify possible connections
- Extract metadata
- Find duplicates
- Transcribe interviews
- Suggest research paths
- Organize information

AI may not:

- Invent evidence
- Create unsupported historical claims
- Remove contradictions
- Hide uncertainty
- Replace human historical judgment

- Constitutional documents
- Architectural decisions
- Standards
- Workflows

- The historical database
- The media archive

Long-Term Vision

The Stokely Universe begins with the preservation of Scott Stokely's legacy.

Over time, it is designed to become a model for preserving broader disc golf history and additional historical collections.

The founding principle is:

«Preserve the evidence. Preserve the reasoning. Preserve the story.»
