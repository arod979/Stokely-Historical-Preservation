# Stokely Universe Constitution v1.0

## Article 1 — Purpose

The Stokely Historical Preservation system exists to preserve, organize, verify, and interpret historical knowledge related to Scott Stokely’s legacy, disc golf history, and future related collections.

The purpose is not merely preservation of objects.

The purpose is preservation of understanding.

---

# Article 2 — Mission Statement

The mission is:

To create a permanent historical knowledge institution that protects authentic stories, preserves evidence, connects relationships, and enables future generations to understand the people, events, and culture that shaped disc golf history.

---

# Article 3 — Authority

The Constitution is the highest governing document of the Stokely Historical Preservation system.

All frameworks, databases, workflows, AI systems, and future technologies must conform to these principles.

---

# Article 4 — Historical Truth Standards

The system must distinguish between:

## Historical Fact

Information supported by reliable evidence.

## Historical Evidence

Materials used to support understanding.

Examples:

- Documents
- Photographs
- Videos
- Interviews
- Publications
- Records

## Historical Interpretation

Reasoned understanding based upon available evidence.

Interpretation must remain connected to evidence.

---

# Article 5 — Unknown Information

Unknown information must be preserved as unknown.

The system must not:

- Fill gaps with assumptions.
- Convert speculation into fact.
- Remove uncertainty for convenience.

Absence of knowledge is itself historical information.

---

# Article 6 — Firsthand Knowledge

Firsthand accounts receive special preservation value because they capture experiences unavailable elsewhere.

However:

Firsthand does not automatically mean complete.

The system preserves:

- The person's account.
- The context of the account.
- Supporting evidence.
- Other perspectives.

---

# Article 7 — Provenance

Every significant historical resource should maintain provenance information.

Including:

- Source origin
- Creator
- Ownership
- Acquisition history
- Modifications
- Verification status

---

# Article 8 — Rights and Stewardship

Historical preservation requires responsible management of:

- Copyright
- Ownership
- Permissions
- Privacy
- Ethical use

Preservation does not eliminate responsibility.

---

# Article 9 — Narrative Responsibility

Stories and public interpretation must emerge from preserved evidence.

Narrative is not created first.

Evidence comes first.

---

# Article 10 — Long-Term Vision

The Stokely Historical Preservation system should support:

- Museum exhibitions
- Educational programs
- Research
- Autobiographical projects
- Public history
- Future institutional collections
